study27 combat micro — completed frozen confirmation
==================================================

The frozen combination cm_combo_aoe_dash passed all seven recorded confirmation
gates. It won 1,074–974 against the original study27 and improved the matched
three-spar panel by 161 wins across 6,144 games. This supports the combined bot
under the tested distribution. It is not a guarantee about the current ladder.

This is a work-only completed-results report. Remote publication and delivery
are controlled by root after its independent final audit. The frozen source,
pending ZIP and existing review bundle have not been changed by this report.

Source and behavior
-------------------
Baseline: Javiops/Cambridge-Battlecode-2026 study27 at commit
c462346d4ace741bfd8f5251fb0e5e2eee69b800.
Only main.cpp and fight.h change; four other source files are byte-identical.

1. AoE edge fallback: the original spell sweep ignored enemies beyond four tiles
   even when a legal ground centre could hit them with an AoE radius. The new
   fallback checks visible live enemies beyond that old cutoff, the real cast
   range and AoE radius, bounds-aware centre selection, spawn exclusion, cast
   readiness and existing cast-mode/fuel gates. Existing nearby casts retain
   priority and the fallback returns after a successful cast.
2. Dash cooldown reach: the original named-melee-dash reach heuristic was always
   three. It now remains three for a dash with cooldown <=1 and for missing or
   unreadable metadata; it becomes ordinary melee reach two for longer known
   cooldowns. Other melee/ranged return values are unchanged. It still does not
   prove path feasibility or exactly forecast dash-plus-walk/initiative.

There are no production edits to draft, parameter values, recall thresholds,
shop, camp selection/capture logic or shared-memory protocol. Different combat
decisions can still indirectly change economic outcomes and trajectories.

Selection and development
-------------------------
The recorded plan screened eleven isolated variants on 53 exposed maps
(nine official plus 44 generated), both sides against original and spar11,
spar12 and spar16: 4,664 new physical games. Nine variants failed promotion and
are excluded from the submitted source. Two advanced to the complete 253-map
development cohort; exact screen and baseline inputs were reused, not counted
as new evidence. Full development added 3,200 physical games. The combination
and direct component ablations added 3,036 physical games.

Full-development direct-original / matched-panel incremental wins:
  AoE alone: 274–232 / +20 (1,262 versus 1,242 of 1,518).
  Dash alone: 260–246 / +9 (1,251 versus 1,242).
  Combination: 272–234 / +28 (1,270 versus 1,242).
The selected combination met the recorded incremental-panel and component-
comparison criteria. Development results are selected/exposed evidence.

The incremental dash evidence is weak: combination versus AoE alone was
258–248, paired-map two-sided p=0.602923. Combination versus dash alone was
264–242, p=0.043285. The fresh gate tested the combination only and does not
independently establish that each component helps on fresh maps.

Frozen confirmation
-------------------
The exact source/WASM was frozen before generating all 1,024 confirmation maps
(seed 2154873391). Map hashes were checked against exposed development inputs.
No candidate swap, tuning or early stopping was based on this cohort. Both
complete stages finished before these mechanism statistics were inspected.

All 14,336 semantic slots correspond to 14,336 distinct physical simulations:
  original versus three fixed spars, both sides: 6,144 games;
  frozen candidate versus original, both sides: 2,048 games;
  frozen candidate versus three spars, both sides: 6,144 games.

Direct original: 1,074 wins, 974 losses, no draws (52.44%). There were 139 maps
with both sides won and 89 with both lost; exact two-sided paired-map sign
p=0.0011275547447593003.

Matched spar panel: 5,050/6,144 wins (82.19%) versus original's 4,889/6,144
(79.57%): +161 wins, +2.6204427083333335 percentage points. Summing both sides
and all three spars within each map yields 221 positive, 138 negative and 665
neutral map deltas; exact two-sided sign p=1.3867384149654957e-05.

Candidate games were 4.961 rounds shorter on average. The positive gems, kills
and champion-damage direction, and fewer deaths/damage taken, also held within
each spar subgroup. This pattern is consistent with improved combat outcomes,
but does not identify the cause of a win or isolate either feature's effect.
Not every map improved: submitted-gem mean deltas were positive on 347 maps,
negative on 285 and equal on 392. Kill deltas were positive on 335, negative on
207 and equal on 482. There are no component activity counters, so these data
cannot count decisions changed by the AoE edge or cooldown branch. Collected
gems can include champion loot; monster damage cannot establish camp captures,
steals or deaths while farming.

Quality and meaningful checks
-----------------------------
All 14,336 confirmation games reported zero actual bot faults and zero observed
reinitializations. The matched 6,144-game mechanism panel had zero paired draft
mismatches. Periodic low-fuel samples were equal: 3,864 for each policy. Caught
SDK errors remain nonzero: 105,705 candidate log samples versus 106,041 original
samples. Logs emit only early errors and periodic later samples; neither this
difference nor zero bot faults establishes an SDK-error-free run or complete
fuel/error observation.

Recorded source/helper validation includes 38 extracted target-helper checks;
six movement fixture runs with 50 checks each; seven target and six movement
scratch full-SDK builds; all eleven registered isolates and final combination
built successfully. Pure transforms checked all 63 target subset requests,
31 movement subsets and 1,953 cross-module compositions with unchanged inputs
and main.cpp/fight.h-only differences. Fixtures check local contracts, not
game efficacy. Exact-control source and WASM matched study27 before experiments.
