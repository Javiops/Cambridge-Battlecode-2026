# Integration defects found and repaired

These are development findings, not additional successful final matches. Every final result
must name the frozen source/module hashes in `source-manifest.json`.

| Finding | Repair | Relevant regression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SDK ability handles constructed while hidden retain a cached metadata error after revelation. | Rebind once from an actually personally visible tile; never infer hidden kit state. | Native lifecycle encounter |
| Dead actors cannot be queried as if they still had a visible live record. | Check life first, preserve own immutable type for dead shopping, and observe respawn freshly. | Native death, wallet/inventory purchase and respawn |
| Repeated live attribute accessors repeat the same expensive host snapshot. | Isolate the pinned official SDK snapshot adapter and reuse one record within a refresh. | Header checks and native lifecycle |
| A failed incumbent bypassed its own failure exclusion and could be selected forever. | Apply suppression to the incumbent shortlist and continuity preference. | Full production farming projection |
| Travel time was treated as clear stagnation before the first hit. | Start clear progress when actually entering Clear; start assembly waiting when entering Assemble. | Full production first-clear arrival |
| Assembling bodies waited outside their useful attack range. | Approach a real contribution tile without a waking hit; check the next response before joining an awake camp. | Full-game activity and production farming |
| Whole-group pressure was charged to self regardless of actual nearest targets. | Evaluate each selected subset's nearest visible tanks and full damage on ties; retain conservative hidden-member and full-duration bounds. | Camp affordability helper checks |
| An unaffordable high-value camp could hide an affordable alternative. | Reserve a coarse solo-affordable alternative and prefer positive-rate ready work over an infeasible assembly lease. | Production farming alternative; fixed geometry cohort |
| Readiness priority could resurrect a candidate whose rate was zeroed by the horizon. | Require a positive feasible rate before replacing the best candidate. | Deadline eligibility projection |
| A free dash landing did not prove a legal intervening path. | Separate range, traversal and landing: walls, enemy spawn, visible occupied tiles and reservations block traversal; holes/altars may be crossed. | Native wall/hole/bend and unique diagonal obstruction cases |
| A pre-action legality query can outlive its original owner budget. | Check owner epoch and funded mutation budget again immediately before every gameplay SDK mutation. | Source gateway review, full-game API/error evidence |
| A rejected action could advertise a newly abandoned camp claim. | Publish the selected claim after acceptance, or on a deliberate hold; clear it on rejection. | Gateway/main-loop source review |

The fixed map list is development integration evidence after these repairs. It is not an
untouched strength holdout. Native fuel pauses, match losses and deliberately partial
strategies remain visible in the final reports; no online submission was made.
