# The 8 new site maps (2026-10-16): how the top 10 play them, what our bot lacks

Same method as the study of the original 9 maps: top-10 vs top-10 games from the public API, replays decoded round by
round, the same metrics for the winner, the loser and us. Full Spanish report and data:
`/mnt/project-files/analysis/new-maps-2026-10-16/README.md`; scripts and per-map tables in
`analysis/new-maps-2026-10-16/` (crawl.py -> details.py -> pick.py -> dl.py/dl2.py -> ext.py -> analyze.py -> report.py).

Sample: 696 top-10 vs top-10 groups since 00:00 UTC; 120 replays on the new maps (15 per map, won by the 3 best teams
on that map), 48 of our recent games vs top-30 teams, 36 replays of old maps for drafts.

Note: since today `match-groups/<id>` returns no `statistics` for public games; replays (`/matches/<id>/bundle`) are
still public (sometimes `{"status":"pending"}` first, then the zip). The site answers 503 under load: one request per 4-5 s.

## Findings (medians, top-10 winners vs us)
- Draft: Cassi&Issac in every winning draft on 14/14 maps checked (old maps too), Stellar in most.
- Gold: winners earn ~2x the loser's gold on all 8 new maps and buy 17-22 times a game (us 9-12).
- stampede: the only gem group is central (360 gems, 17400 hp). Winners stay away until r350-475, farm ~9000 gold
  (56 gold captures), then take it by force. We go there at r53 with P_NOGOLD, 1 buy, 27 deaths, 0 gems.
- contest: same pattern, longer (11700 gold, first gem r1337). The online bot scores 0 (P_MONO not uploaded).
- eight: a fight map with spawn_path 46 (our fight threshold is 45); we bank faster early, then die 11 times.
- fragment, swirl: same draft as the winners; we lose by dying (17-18 deaths vs 7-9): micro, not macro.
- pilgrimage, ambush, rushbait-v2: farming maps; small gaps, winners pick up gold clusters on the way.
