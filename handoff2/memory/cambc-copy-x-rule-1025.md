Example given only as illustration (not a reference): everyone focuses the first target; if we focus the first target too and still lose, maybe they win because they have more gold / different items / hit from outside vision. "Hay que ir deep, no quedarse en cosas superficiales."

**Why:** today's pieces moved their own mechanism and did not move wins (o103 finish-the-group, o105 Vesper kite, f107 elk admission, damage shop f109/f110 → more deaths to rivals). Copying a top-10 behaviour without its enabler fails.

**How to apply:** when a copied X does not win, stop iterating on X. Condition on doing X: compare them and us in the situations where both do X, and find the variable that still differs and explains the outcome (gold, items, range, vision, numbers, timing, who is hurt). Keep going one level deeper until a variable explains the outcome difference; only then build the piece, and build it on that variable. Coordinator relays this to every line.
