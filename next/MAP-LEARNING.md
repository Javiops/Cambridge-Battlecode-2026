## Controls against overfitting

- No new map-name or opponent-name strategy cases. V6 changes a general movement fallback; v5 used map area but was rejected for lack of progress.
- Use all 35 official maps, 100 prior generated development layouts and 16 newly seeded large development layouts (72–100 tiles per side), with both seats. Padded layouts stress area; they do not cover all possible large-map topologies.
- Seventy new confirmation layouts were reserved: 35 standard, 35 large. **Zero confirmation games were played.** Neither failed candidate was tuned on confirmation outcomes, and no consumed old cohort was reused.
- Keep exact source/model identity, isolated changes, fixed thresholds and a new gate before any stable upload. Improvements smaller than the measured calibration noise remain unproven.

Next test one isolated objective-execution hypothesis: after healthy allies assemble, maintain damage on the same gem-producing camp until reward collection, disengagement conditions or a bounded timeout. Diagnose failures with collection/deposit curves and team deaths, then screen on generated single-objective and multi-objective geometries. Separately profile native movement costs before changing another global fuel threshold. Preserve wins on ordinary multi-camp maps.
