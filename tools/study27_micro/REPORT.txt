study27 safe-farming and team v39 experiment report
===================================================

reproduction tooling, not a stronger submission or a production bot patch.

What was tested
---------------
22 distinct evaluated policies: six live-camp threat guards, two global
cooldown reservations, five narrower follow-ups, two generic held-camp
re-entry windows, and all seven nonempty combinations of the three active
v39 components. The two raw reentry16/32 preparations were never played;
only their separately pinned active-geometry wrappers were evaluated.

Each candidate plays original study27 and the three fixed sparring bots from
both sides. The original's results against identical spar/map/side inputs
form the matched comparison. We count a repeated deterministic physical
input once, even when it supplies multiple stage or comparator views.

The general development set is official9 + existing maps_gen44 + maps_dev200
(253 maps). These are previously exposed selection data, not a holdout.
The five narrow policies first used the official+gen 53-map subset. Dash was
advanced despite a neutral panel under a recorded adaptive exception; its
full 253-map result was negative. No promising candidate remained to combine
across policy families. All three v39 components and their combinations were
tested, including the exact full uploaded version.

Corners coverage
----------------
The v39 tactics activate on the exact 71x71/5-altar/44-wall fingerprint. The
general fresh-map generator has even altar counts, so cannot validate them.
We therefore used the official Corners map plus 64 new Corners-family
topologies from seed1749920311, generated before their outcomes were read.
The final development generator alternates 32 long spawn paths (52-64) and
32 inset paths (26-42), includes central gem hotspots, and varies team size
3/4/5. Native integrity, expansion, reachability, symmetry and D4-normalized
non-bush geometry uniqueness were checked. A counted original/original
probe confirms caster draft plus central hold on an inset map.

An initial 64-map V1 cohort was discovered before games to have only long
paths, missing the caster gate. It was not played. V2 corrected this coverage
before Corners outcomes. These conditioned maps are a controlled activated
domain, not a sample of all possible tournament maps.

maps with a positive/negative sum of matched deltas, not independent games.
All p-values below are descriptive development statistics after adaptation
and many comparisons. They are not confirmatory proof or adjusted tests.

Interpretation and retention
----------------------------
All eight broad guards, both generic re-entry policies, and all seven Corners
sets have negative matched spar-panel point estimates on their full panels.
The full dash follow-up is 251-255 against study27 and loses seven panel wins.
No policy has a positive full-panel estimate that justifies promotion to a
frozen candidate. The exact full v39 is 64-66 against study27 on the Corners
cohort; its spar panel is 257/390 versus study27's298/390 (41 fewer wins).
The flee-only policy wins74-56 against study27 but loses28 matched panel wins:
self-play alone would choose an unsupported change.

On the general253-map panel, fullv39 is254-252 against study27, and ties its
1242/1518 spar score. Outside official Corners its aggregate direct score is
252/504 and spar deltas are zero. A few individual outcomes differ, so tactical
gating must not be described as identical play or byte-equivalent runtime.

The planned independent1024 general and256 Corners confirmations were NOT
generated or run: no candidate passed development selection. They are not
missing successful validation. Production bots, recall, draft, purchases,
wards and shared-memory protocol receive no release change from this PR.

Mechanism findings and next focus
--------------------------------
Broad guards can repeatedly wait, abandon already damaged camps, or reject an
engage because one optional landing is unsafe. Retained periodic counters
support repeated waiting as a possible failure mode; they do not prove stolen
camps or causal losses. Additional SDK inspections also have a runtime cost.
The spell/damage forecast and buddy distance are heuristics, not exact combat
or reachable-support calculations. The pre-turn spell sweep already happens
before the farming gate, so this late guard cannot reserve all earlier casts.

v39 contains three distinct active rules: held-camp lone-entry exclusion;
buddy relaxation of the existing danger exclusion for nonheld camps; and a
45% HP Corners flee threshold. Central hold stays enabled in the actual ZIP.
Its buddy count includes injured/channeling living allies near the champion,
not demonstrated combat support near the camp. The generic re-entry attempt
uses healthier available support and exempts fresh rewards, but also fails to
improve the matched panel. Held geometry additionally depends on the roster.

The useful next research target is action-level fight/support readiness and
camp-reward handoff measurement before another broad farm veto. Existing
aggregate gems/deaths cannot identify captures, steals or deaths while farming.
Explicit decision counters and replay samples would help distinguish useful
retreats from repeated waiting. This is a proposed next experiment, not an
implemented or validated improvement.

Validation and limits
---------------------
Candidate source/WASM/runtime/map identities were pinned before each stage.
Source transforms, representative C++ helpers, composition and portable
24-feature routing were checked independently. All24 preparation features
match registered source hashes, including the two unplayed raw preparations.
The portable harness supports28 corners/re-entry source compositions; those
composition checks are not28 evaluated bot policies or extra matches.

No CLI-reported bot fault or repeated initialization was observed in completed
stages. Caught SDK exceptions DO exist, including original study27 (visibility,
post-move cast and unreachable errors). Logging is sampled, and no-fault is
not no-error or proof of no unlogged fuel shortage. Retained replays are sampled,
not a complete native replay archive of every game. See independent-audit.txt,
mechanism-notes.txt and the exported physical-game audit for exact scope.

The opponent panel is three older fixed sparring bots. These results do not
establish strength against current online opponents or unseen tournament maps.
The Corners sample deliberately conditions a named-map fingerprint and central
hotspot; it cannot prove broad generalization. The negative selection is not
a claim that every conceivable threat-aware strategy is worse.

Reproduction
------------
Keep the bench/patch/compare modules together and follow README.txt. They
reconstruct pinned sources from local Git history without changing tracked
bots or submitting anything. The accompanying map archive and per-game
outcomes/provenance allow the exact evaluated panels to be reconstructed.
The result-export audit documents reused deterministic keys rather than adding
them to the count. Full local statistics and selected native replays were
retained for audit; the compact GitHub evidence intentionally omits their bulk.
