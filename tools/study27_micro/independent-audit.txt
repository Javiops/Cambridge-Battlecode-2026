Decision
--------
Retain no proposed production change from these completed experiments. Every
fully evaluated generic policy and all seven Corners component sets have a
negative matched spar-panel point estimate. A small self-play lead alone does
not meet the recorded selection rule. This does not prove every idea is always
harmful; it means these candidates have not earned promotion on this evidence.
Publish the negative experiment and reproducible tools for review. Keep stable
study27 separate from uploaded v39, whose superiority was never assumed.

Cohort coverage and limits
-------------------------
V1 geometric maps were never used in matches. Code review found their paths
missed the caster draft gate, so V2 was specified before Corners outcomes.
V2 has32 far paths(52-64),32 inset paths(26-42), exact71x71/five altars/44walls,
at least four native central gem groups, full camp/altar reachability and64
unique D4-normalized nonbush geometries. These are a deliberately conditioned
Corners-fingerprint/hotspot distribution, not all maps. Geometric eligibility
and path gates do not guarantee the actual roster or hold activation; the
separate parent-run hold probe is not independently assessed in this report.

The generic fresh generator always creates an even altar count, so it cannot
validate Corners-specific behavior. No confirmation cohort was generated by
this audit. With no qualifying candidate, do not describe selection data as
a successful independent confirmation or claim an improved submission ZIP.

Caught SDK errors remain
------------------------

samples across1518 games; active16 logged27074 and active32 logged27078 across

destination) and45(cannot act) also remain. Do not label the bots API-error-free,
