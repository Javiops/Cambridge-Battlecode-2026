# Native bank policy regression

Run `run_native_bank.py --cli PATH --project ARENA --source bots/tactics-v1/src --output NEW_DIRECTORY`. It requires the pinned official SDK2.2.2 and native engine0.0.2, verifies the committed controlled map, and preserves source/module/replay/statistics hashes.

Both actual SDK owners run six explicitly projected policy cases:

- A committed delivery selects a safer legal adjacent ring tile instead of overriding that useful relocation with unrelated work.
- The real coordinator's necessary-recovery branch records the failed delivery window through its production hook.
- A healed/returned projection with no fresh view of that recorded window retains the finite uncertainty backoff and considers another altar.
- An accepted active tribute retains the planned next job without entering it.
- The later completed-bank observation input enters a finite named Reenter mission; normal farming still revalidates its route, crew and action.
- A timed quote with128 personally fresh projected job records and approximately65,000 fuel entry preserves the30,000 action reserve. Expensive fixture registry construction is sliced across earlier owner opportunities and excluded from the policy sample.

No projected gameplay action is executed, and the bag/channel transitions are fixture inputs. This proves actual production helper/coordinator activation under the initialized SDK, not physical movement, real tribute payout, exhaustive threat safety, or competitive strength. The separate native cycle regression covers actual engine payout timing; full-bot games remain required after the final source freeze.

The next-job search has a bounded geometric scan and at most two exact cached viability queries. A missing or unscanned continuation stays unpriced; it is not a zero-cost return or a global best-job claim. Ring forecasting also examines at most two cheap-ranked neighbors and keeps a30,000 fuel floor. A failed-window TTL is an initial backoff, not proof that an altar is permanently unsafe.
